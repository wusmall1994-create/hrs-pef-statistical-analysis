# Analysis amendment before upgraded effect estimation

Date: 2026-09-09. This is a post hoc manuscript upgrade, not a preregistration.

Primary question: association of baseline age-, sex- and height-relative PEF with first subsequent report of physician-diagnosed chronic lung disease, and incremental predictive information. No mediation or causal disease-onset claims.

1. Lock each HRS person's first baseline meeting age 50–80, valid PEF/height, no reported lung disease, positive weight and valid survey design, before checking follow-up. CHARLS primary baseline remains 2015. All-baseline sex-specific PEF reference fits are frozen before restriction to observed outcomes or covariate-complete samples.
2. Reproduce archived sample/event counts, then report new selection flow and change in participants/baselines. Do not reuse cached imputation results.
3. Survey-weighted modified Poisson primary models retain the original covariates. Use complete cases as the main analysis; 20 imputations of covariates only among observed outcome, valid exposure baseline participants. Report actual model n/events. Recompute censoring weights on the same locked baseline; deaths and nonresponse count as unobserved. Death-inclusive composite is a sensitivity analysis, not competing-risk modelling.
4. Compare core and expanded adjustment on identical complete-case samples for (a) maximum grip, (b) baseline doctor and hospital contact, (c) both, (d) smoking intensity where available. Scale grip per 10 kg; current cigarettes/day per 10, with zero for non-current smokers and separate smoking status. No data-driven covariate selection.
5. HRS raw symptoms require both local-file availability and questionnaire eligibility assessment. Structurally absent symptoms are not coded negative or imputed. Only fit an exploratory symptoms model if adequate sample/events and identifiable coefficients permit it; otherwise document exclusion.
6. Retain frozen-baseline lagged and repeated-positive outcome checks, alternative exposure thresholds, CHARLS effort/repeatability checks, published reference definitions, subgroup and spline analyses. Recalculate affected analyses; do not copy old numerical results into new tables.
7. Incremental prediction: cohort-specific weighted logistic models using age, age squared, sex, height, smoking, BMI and HRS wave, versus the same plus continuous PEF residual; second comparison adds grip and baseline doctor/hospital contact to both models. Five repeats of fivefold PSU-grouped cross-validation; PEF reference regression estimated only in each training fold. Mean repeat-wise weighted AUC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope. No diagnostic accuracy or decision-curve claims and no external validation claim. Fixed fold assignments shared across paired models. Final full-data coefficients and transformations exported separately; participant data remain in work only.
8. Figures: R backend matching original workflow. Figure 1 sample selection; Figure 2 core/expanded association estimates with 95% CIs; Figure 3 continuous dose response; Figure 4 held-out predictive calibration. White background; CHARLS blue/HRS orange; 180 mm width; PDF and 600 dpi TIFF plus preview PNG; numeric sources accompany figures.
9. Manuscript: English Research article, Respiratory Research; preserve verified source references and author details. Rewrite results to match upgraded output, distinguish exploratory additions, and disclose limitations and code-version boundary. Supplement and STROBE updated. Deliver clean DOCX plus marked-change summary, source tables, scripts and figure files. No participant data in deliverables.

Terminology: peak expiratory flow (PEF); first reported physician diagnosis of chronic lung disease; China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS); Health and Retirement Study (HRS); risk ratio (RR); risk difference in percentage points; internal cross-validation. Never substitute incident COPD, screening accuracy, clinical external validation or causal mechanism.

Amendment after the first two prediction comparisons: add a third, full-clinical comparator containing all core association covariates plus grip and healthcare contact. Reason: the simpler comparators do not include all available clinical information. Retain and report all three comparator suites; do not choose a suite on the basis of its increment. These prediction analyses remain exploratory/post hoc.

Prediction implementation correction: a rare CHARLS education category was absent in a training fold. For the full-clinical prediction comparator only, use four substantively defined categories (codes 1–3 below primary; 4 primary; 5 middle; 6+ high school or above). Do not change association-model education categories. The adjustment prevents unseen category prediction failure and is not outcome-driven selection.
